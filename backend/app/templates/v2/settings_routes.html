Ordered fallback routes

DEFAULT is the chain used by every research step unless that step has an explicit override.

Position is strict order: position 0 is tried first, then each fallback after an unavailable, unhealthy, authentication, rate-limit, or transient failure. Route slots are not weighted.

A provider slot dynamically includes all of that provider's active, compatible models. Credential weight only balances healthy keys inside one provider; weight 2 receives about twice the selections of weight 1.
